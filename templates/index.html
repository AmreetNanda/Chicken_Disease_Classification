Chicken Disease Classification

Coccidiosis

A parasitic disease of the intestinal tract of chickens caused by protozoa. Symptoms include diarrhea, weight loss, and weakness.

New Castle Disease

A contagious viral disease affecting chickens, leading to respiratory distress, nervous signs, and reduced egg production.

Salmonella

A bacterial infection that can spread from chickens to humans. Symptoms in chickens include diarrhea and reduced growth.
