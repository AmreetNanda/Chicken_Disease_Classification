🐔 Chicken Disease Classification

Coccidiosis

Symptoms: Diarrhea, weight loss, weakness.

Treatment: Anticoccidial medication, good hygiene.

New Castle Disease

Symptoms: Respiratory distress, nervous signs, reduced egg production.

Treatment: Vaccination, supportive care.

Salmonella

Symptoms: Diarrhea, reduced growth, lethargy.

Treatment: Antibiotics, proper sanitation.

Healthy

Symptoms: Normal behavior and appearance.

Treatment: None needed; maintain good care.
